Automated Growth Agency: contract vs reality

Service Agreement & SOW signed Jun 9 2026, $2,000/mo plus Meta ad spend. Checked live on Nov 17 2026 in Meta Ad Library, the landing page and the AGA CRM (app.automatedgrowthagency.com). Hover a row to see the full evidence.
Short answer: the ads ARE running now (since Oct 23). Results were close to the promise in July and August (about 20 paid deposits a month), then went to zero from Sep 13 to Oct 22, and are now only 2 to 4 a month. What is "not connected" is only the CRM's ad-reporting link, not the ads. They do the basics (ads, landing page, automatic texts, call dialer, deposits), but are not delivering the results now: 6 paid deposits since Oct 23 versus a promised 20 prepaid and attended patients a month, follow-up stuck on day 1, only 1 future appointment booked, and the ads have not been changed or tested in 3 weeks. Several promised items (no-show system, reviews, referrals, reactivation) are not set up.

What I'd do: take it in-house, in this order

Why: the system itself works. In July and August it brought about 20 paid deposits a month. What fell apart is AGA's running of it: a 6 week stop, the same ad for 4 weeks, and leads left on day 1. Today we pay $2,000 a month plus at least $1,500 in ad spend for 2 to 4 deposits, roughly $700+ per deposit. Running it ourselves costs about $100 to $300 a month in tools (our own GoHighLevel account, Square, texting), and the new ads are already designed.

  1. Secure what is ours first (this week). Make sure Diase is the admin of the Meta business, ad account, pixel, Facebook page and Instagram. Export every contact, lead and opportunity from the AGA CRM. Ask AGA for the monthly reports the contract promises. Do this before telling them we are leaving.
  2. Build our copy (about 2 weeks). Our own CRM with the same pieces: landing page with the $30 prepay, instant text plus call-within-5-minutes, a 7 day follow-up, appointment reminders, STOP handling. Launch the refreshed ads (4:5 and video, several offers, Spanish versions).
  3. Decide who answers new leads. This is the one thing that decides success. Fast calls during opening hours by the front desk, an AI text assistant after hours. Leads that wait an hour mostly do not book.
  4. Switch over. When our page takes its first deposits, give AGA written notice (the contract allows cancel any time) and move the ad budget across. Saves about $2,000 a month.

If someone has to make calls and nobody at the spa can, the fallback is to keep AGA for 30 more days with a written fix list (new ads weekly, follow-up past day 1, monthly reports) and cut them if deposits stay under 10.
