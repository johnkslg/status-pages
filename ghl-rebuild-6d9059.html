The pipeline

  1. New lead: form submitted, nobody has spoken to them yet.
  2. Contacted: they replied or picked up.
  3. Deposit paid, consult booked.
  4. Showed.
  5. Bought: treatment or package sold.
  6. No-show.
  7. Nurture: no reply after 7 days, kept for monthly offers.
  8. Lost or opted out.

Tags: one per offer (underarms-199, brazilian-600, unlimited-lhr-949, face-neck-497, fotona-facial, baggy-eye, hyperpigmentation), plus source-meta, source-website, spanish, deposit-paid, no-show, client, review-asked, do-not-contact. Fields: offer, preferred language, treatment area, deposit paid date, consult date, last treatment date, referred by, ad name.

The nine workflows and their messages

Texts only go out 9am to 8pm Tampa time. Every sequence stops the moment the person replies, books or opts out. Messages are drafts for the owner to edit; a Spanish version of each is needed because many leads have Spanish names.

1. New lead: reply in under a minute

Right away, text
Hi {{first name}}, it's Diase at Beauty Empire Aesthetics in Tampa. I got your request for {{offer}}. Want me to hold a spot for you this week? Reply here or book at {{booking link}}.
Right away, to staff
New lead: {{name}}, {{offer}}, {{phone}}. Call within 5 minutes.
Right away, email
Subject: Your {{offer}} at Beauty Empire. Short note with what is included, the address (5537 Sheldon Road, Suite L), and the booking button.
5 minutes, no reply
Task for the front desk to call. If nobody answers, a voicemail and this text: "Just tried calling about your {{offer}}. When is a good time to reach you?"

2. Seven-day follow-up (replaces the 14 "Day 1 to Day 7" stages)

Day 1 evening
Hi {{first name}}, still want the {{offer}}? We have openings {{two days}}. A $30 deposit holds your time and comes off your treatment.
Day 2
Quick question: is it timing, price, or just not sure it is right for you? Happy to answer anything.
Day 3, call task, then
Here is what a first visit looks like: about 30 minutes, we check your skin, answer questions, and you decide. Book here: {{booking link}}
Day 5
{{first name}}, we only keep the {{offer}} price open for a limited number of spots this month. Want one?
Day 7
Last note from me. If now is not the time, no problem. Reply LATER and I will check back next month.
Reply LATER (workflow 2b)
Tag "check back later", a short "Got it" text, then 30 days later a check-back text with the booking link.
After day 7
Moves to Nurture.

3. Booked: confirmation and reminders

On booking
You're booked, {{first name}}: {{day, time}} at Beauty Empire Aesthetics, 5537 Sheldon Road, Suite L, Tampa. Your $30 deposit is applied to your treatment. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule.
24 hours before
See you tomorrow at {{time}}. Reply C to confirm. Need to move it? {{reschedule link}}
2 hours before
We are ready for you at {{time}}. Parking is right out front. Call (813) 361-9992 if you are running late.

4. No-show recovery (switched off in the agency account)

30 minutes after a missed visit
Hi {{first name}}, we missed you today. Everything OK? Your deposit is still good if you rebook this week: {{booking link}}
Next day
Call task for the front desk, then a second text two days later.

5. Opt-out handling (switched off in the agency account)

STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, QUIT or END: do-not-contact is set, every sequence ends, the lead moves to Lost. HighLevel's built-in opt-out stays on as well. This one is required by the carriers.

6. After the visit: review request

2 hours after "Showed"
Thank you for coming in, {{first name}}! How was your visit, 1 to 5?
Reply 4 or 5
That makes our day. Would you share it on Google? {{review link}}
Reply 1 to 3
I am sorry to hear that. Diase will call you personally today. (Alert to the owner.)

7. Client rebooking

Laser clients get a text when the next session is due (4 to 6 weeks by area). Package clients get a note when 1 session is left.

8. Reactivation of the 2,900 old contacts

Two texts a week apart, sent in batches of 200 a day once the number is registered. About $70 in text fees. Runs only with the owner's go-ahead on the exact wording.

9. Referral

30 days after a purchase: "Know someone who would love this? Send them this link and you both get $25 off." Wording and amount for the owner to set.
