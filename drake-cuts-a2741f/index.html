Drake's session, cut into four short videos

Four vertical cuts for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, made from the three raw recordings Drake Lando sent of his first DreamForge session. They are separate from his own full edit.

Every cut opens on its funniest moment, then rewinds to the start of his session: opening DreamForge and meeting Forge, talking the game into being, Forge building it, then playing it. The purple tag on his camera shows which part you are in.

His camera sits on top and the game underneath, both shown in full. Captions sit on the line between the two, clear of the TikTok buttons. The only sound is his mic and the game's voices.

Building the game took about 15 minutes. The cuts put "About 15 minutes later" on screen rather than making it look instant.

Each cut has three openings to test against each other. The video uses the first one.
