Running it ourselves: what it would cost

AGA's system is a white-labelled GoHighLevel (HighLevel) account. Ours would be the same software bought direct. Rates read from HighLevel's own pricing and help pages on Nov 17 2026 (AI rates updated Nov 10). Hover a row for the math.
Short answer: about $150 to $200 a month all in, including texting, the call dialer, an AI text assistant and an AI phone answerer. That is about $1,800 a month less than AGA's $2,000 fee. Meta ad spend is the same either way. One-off setup is about $100.

Lean = plan, texts, dialer only. Full = everything including AI. 4x volume = about 400 new leads a month, with Unlimited plan. Today's volume is 94 leads in 30 days.

One-off costs

  • Reactivating the 2,900 old contacts with a 2-text sequence: about $70 (5,800 segments).
  • Text registration with the carriers for our own number: $22.50 brand fee, plus a wait of days to a couple of weeks. The registration AGA did does not carry over.
  • Landing pages, workflows and calendars have to be rebuilt in our account unless AGA shares its template (ask them). Our time or a freelancer for a few days.

Things worth knowing

  • Skip the $50 and $97 "AI Employee" plans for now. Pay-per-use AI costs us about $30 a month at today's volume. The $97 plan only wins past roughly 1,000 AI call minutes a month.
  • Square fees stay the same (about 2.9% + 30 cents per $30 deposit, so roughly $1.17 each). Not in the table.
  • Meta ad spend is separate and unchanged, at least $1,500 a month today.
  • The real cost is a person. Fast call-backs decide whether deposits happen. AI can text and answer after hours, but someone at the spa still has to take the booked consults. That is not in the $150 to $200.
  • All usage comes out of a prepaid wallet that auto-recharges, so spend is visible and capped by us.
